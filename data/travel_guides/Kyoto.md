
# Kyoto Travel Guide

## Fushimi Inari Shrine
Fushimi Inari Shrine is famous for its thousands of red
torii gates. Visitors can walk through the shrine's
mountain trails. Exploring the full route can take several
hours.

## Arashiyama
Arashiyama is a scenic district in western Kyoto. It is
known for its bamboo grove, Togetsukyo Bridge, and
nearby temples.

## Local Transportation
Kyoto has buses, trains, and subway lines. Travelers
should check current routes and schedules before visiting
attractions.

## Practical Travel Advice
Wear comfortable walking shoes because many attractions
involve walking and stairs. Check attraction opening
hours before visiting.
